The Trade-In Program is an automated passive growth pool. You invest a minimum of ₱1,000 to receive a total return of 120% (including original capital) over 12 days. Payouts are credited to your Trade-In Cycle wallet every 3 days (4 equal cycles of 30% each).

The minimum withdrawal is only ₱100 per request. Processing fees are tiered to reward larger withdrawals: 12% for ₱100–₱1,000; 10% for ₱1,001–₱5,000; and only 8% for ₱5,001 and above. Payouts can be sent to GCash, Maya, GoTyme, MariBank, or UnionBank.
